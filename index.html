Hide unwanted YouTube recommendations using category presets and specific topics you choose. Works on Home and beside a playing video.

Early preview. Chrome Web Store submission is being prepared; there is no published store installation link yet.

Choose what to filter

Fourteen presets cover topics such as gaming, music, sports and creator drama. Add custom words or phrases, and pause filtering whenever you want. The popup follows your browser's light or dark preference.

How the current preview works

Your filters stay in local browser storage. The preview checks recommendation titles and channel names, and currently makes additional requests to YouTube for category and tag metadata. These requests send video IDs and network information to YouTube; see the privacy policy. No developer analytics or AI service is used.

Metadata access is under review before store submission. Detection can miss videos with unavailable or inaccurate metadata, and topic matching can also hide educational discussion of a blocked topic.
